Overview

What is the Nervous System?

The nervous system is the body's communication and control network. It receives information, processes it, and coordinates responses.

⚡

Communication

Neurons transmit electrical and chemical signals that allow different parts of the body to communicate.

🧠

Processing

The brain and spinal cord process information received from the senses and determine appropriate responses.

❤️

Coordination

The nervous system coordinates movement, heartbeat, breathing, digestion, and many other functions.

Organization

Two Major Divisions

The nervous system is broadly divided into the central and peripheral nervous systems.

01 / CENTRAL

Central Nervous System

The CNS is the main processing center of the nervous system.

  • Brain
  • Spinal cord
  • Processes sensory information
  • Controls many body responses
02 / PERIPHERAL

Peripheral Nervous System

The PNS connects the central nervous system with the rest of the body.

  • Cranial nerves
  • Spinal nerves
  • Sensory pathways
  • Motor pathways
Anatomy

Nervous System Structure

The brain and spinal cord form the central nervous system, while nerves extend throughout the body.

BRAIN
Cellular Level

Meet the Neuron

Neurons are specialized cells that receive, process, and transmit information using electrical and chemical signals.

A typical neuron contains several specialized structures that work together to transmit signals.

Dendrites
Receive signals from other cells.
Cell Body
Contains the nucleus and maintains the neuron's functions.
Axon
Carries electrical signals away from the cell body.
Axon Terminals
Communicate with other cells through chemical signals.
Functions

What Does It Control?

The nervous system participates in almost every activity of the human body.

👁 Sensation

Detects information such as light, sound, touch, temperature, and pain.

🏃 Movement

Controls voluntary muscle movement and helps coordinate body position.

❤️ Autonomic Control

Helps regulate involuntary functions such as heart rate and digestion.

🧠 Thinking

Supports learning, memory, reasoning, decision-making, and consciousness.

😊 Emotions

Brain networks participate in emotional processing and behavioral responses.

⚡ Reflexes

Some rapid protective responses can occur through reflex pathways.
